---
title: "Preface: Why This Book Matters"
---

For decades, business needs have evolved faster than the software built to support them. The obstacle was never a shortage of ideas. It was the time, cost, complexity, and risk of implementation. The result has been organisational inertia: valuable knowledge trapped inside legacy systems, and adaptation accepted as a routine cost of doing business.

*The Genie in the Bottle* argues that AI is beginning to rewrite this equation. Its significance is not that it writes code, but that it lowers the cost of understanding existing systems, translating new needs into working changes, connecting those changes, and testing them. Work that once demanded months of specialist effort can increasingly be accelerated, allowing more people with real knowledge of a problem to help build the solution.

The potential is an organisation able to adapt faster than before, where IT becomes less of a constraint on progress and more of a catalyst for it.
